Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). To cancel, manage your subscription directly in the store where you purchased it:

Manage iOS subscriptions · Manage Android subscriptions

How do I delete my account?

Open the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your data within 30 days. If you can't access the app, email privacy@lalisa.app from your registered address.

The AI scan returned wrong results.

AI scanning is a best-effort estimate based on the image you provide. For the most accurate results: take a clear, well-lit photo of one meal at a time, and edit the macros manually if needed. You can also report inaccurate scans through the app's Feedback screen — your reports help us improve the model.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link within a few minutes. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive. Still stuck? Email support@lalisa.app.

I want a refund.

Refunds are handled by the store you purchased from:
