Delete Your Account

You're in control of your data.

You can permanently delete your Lalisa account at any time. This page explains how to do it from inside the app, what data is removed, and what we're legally required to retain.

Heads up — this is permanent.

Once your account is deleted, your meal logs, scans, and profile data cannot be recovered. If you only want to take a break, signing out of the app is enough.

How to delete your account

The fastest way is from inside the Lalisa app:

  1. Open the Lalisa app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile (bottom-right tab).
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm by typing DELETE when prompted.

You'll be signed out immediately. Your account and personal data are removed from our active database right away. Encrypted backups containing residual copies are purged automatically within 90 days.

Cancel your subscription first

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active App Store or Play Store subscription — those are managed by the store, not by us. To avoid further charges, cancel before deleting:

What happens to your data

Deleted immediately

  • Your account & profile
  • Meal & scan history
  • Daily logs & macros
  • Saved preferences
  • Sign-in identifiers

Retained temporarily or permanently

  • Encrypted database backups — purged within 90 days (Supabase automatic)
  • Subscription transaction records — kept by Apple / Google / RevenueCat for tax & legal compliance
  • Anonymized usage logs with no personal identifiers

For a full breakdown, see our Privacy Policy.

Can't access the app?

If you've lost access (forgot password, lost device, etc.) and can't delete the account from inside the app, email us instead:

privacy@lalisa.app
Email from the address registered with your Lalisa account so we can verify your identity. We'll process your request within 7 business days and confirm by reply.
